# Code of Conduct

We want participation in Session Bridge to be respectful and useful for everyone. Treat people with courtesy, make room for different backgrounds and experience levels, and focus criticism on ideas and work rather than people.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, intimidation, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable in project spaces. If a conversation becomes difficult, pause and address the issue calmly. Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to keep the community constructive.

For a conduct concern, contact the project through [GitHub](https://github.com/pma1999/session-bridge). Please include enough context to understand what happened while avoiding unnecessary disclosure of private information.
